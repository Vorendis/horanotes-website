Why the name Hora

Jyotish has three branches. Ganita does the calculation, Samhita reads events in the wider world, and Hora reads the life of one person from their birth chart. Hora is the work of this practice.

In the Brihat Jataka, Varahamihira records that the word comes from ahorātra, a day and a night, with its first and last syllables dropped. A hora is also an hour of that day, ruled in turn by one of the seven grahas, as the dial above shows.

Questions

Which system of astrology do you follow?

Vedic astrology, or Jyotish, in the Parashari tradition, with the Brihat Parashara Hora Shastra as the core text.

What do I need before the session?

Your date of birth, the exact time of birth and the place you were born. A birth certificate or hospital record is the best source for the time. If you only know it roughly, mention that when you book.

Can I ask more questions after the session?

Questions are answered during the consultation itself, so bring all of them. You keep the recording and the written notes, and you can return to them whenever you like.

Which languages can the consultation be in?

Hindi or English. Choose the one you are most comfortable in when you book.
